Last updated: 27 October 2026

Magic Colour is a free game for children, made by Gerard Halligan. It is designed so that it needs nothing from you or your child except a screen to play on.

What we collect

Nothing. Magic Colour does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, no account, no email address, no name, no location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone or camera use, and no advertising ID.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads and no in-app purchases. There are no analytics, trackers, cookies for tracking, or third-party code. The game does not send any information about you or how you play to anyone.

What is saved on your device

To remember where you were, the game saves which pictures you have coloured, how far you got in each picture, the stickers you have earned, and your choices such as sound on or off, the colouring mode and the brush size. This is stored only on your own device (in the app's local storage). It never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Magic Colour can also work without internet. After the first visit the game files are kept on your device so the game can open offline.

You can delete everything at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Magic Colour → Storage → Clear storage), or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Magic Colour is made for children, including young children, and follows the principle of collecting no data at all. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties. There are no links, chat, or social features in the game.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
